Capture protocol and shortcuts
Cameras
  • One side camera per leg (or one camera, two trials at the same speed) and one rear camera.
  • 200 fps or more, shutter 1/1000 s or faster, bright flicker-free light.
  • Fixed, levelled tripods on marked floor positions, square to the plane, 3–5 m away, zoomed in.
  • Calibrate once on the belt (tape marks for side views, belt width for the rear) and save each camera as a station.
Markers
  • Side: acromion, greater trochanter, lateral femoral condyle, lateral malleolus, heel and 5th metatarsal head on the shoe (same height).
  • Rear: left and right PSIS, mid-thigh, posterior knee and mid-calcaneus.
Running
  • Standard familiarisation, fixed test speed, 1% gradient, shoes recorded.
  • A short standing clip in each side view for the foot-angle reference.
  • At least 10 consecutive strides per view.
Workflow
  1. Session details, then open each video (MP4, MOV or AVI).
  2. Check the station calibration or calibrate (C).
  3. Process all views, or per view: Auto-label (A), Track (T), Events (E), Calculate (Enter).
  4. Check every contact and toe-off frame by frame and nudge with [ and ].
  5. Save the session (Ctrl+S). It is also the file you load as the previous session at a retest.
  6. Report (Ctrl+R): pick up to three key findings, write the summary, print or save as PDF.
